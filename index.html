      About

      Book & Notes Vault is a small catalog for the books you own or plan to read. Tag them, track how many pages are waiting for you, keep reading notes on the Notes page, and search everything with real regular expressions. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

      It's built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules, with no frameworks, as a project on responsive, accessible front-end development.
